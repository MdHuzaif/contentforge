# ContentForge AI Standard Operating Procedures (SOP): Content Guidelines

## 1. Blog Writing Best Practices
- **Hook in First 100 Words**: Capture reader attention immediately with a compelling statistic, provocative question, or strong value proposition within the first 100 words.
- **Heading Hierarchy (H2/H3)**: Use exactly one H1 title. Organize subtopics using H2 headings for main sections and H3 headings for detailed subsections. Never skip heading levels.
- **Short Paragraphs**: Keep paragraphs concise (2-4 sentences max) to enhance scannability and mobile reading experience.
- **Tone & Voice**: Authoritative yet accessible, objective, helpful, and engaging.

## 2. SEO Guidelines
- **Keyword Density**: Maintain primary keyword density between 0.5% and 2.5% naturally throughout the text. Avoid keyword stuffing.
- **Meta Description**: Craft compelling meta descriptions under 160 characters containing the primary keyword and a clear call-to-action (CTA).
- **Internal Linking**: Reference related articles or topic clusters naturally within anchor text to reinforce topical authority.
- **Alt Text & Media**: Ensure all images and visual assets include descriptive alt text containing relevant keywords.

## 3. Content Structure Templates
- **Introduction**: Hook, problem statement, and article roadmap.
- **Body Sections (H2/H3)**: In-depth analysis, actionable advice, and conceptual breakdowns.
- **Comparison Table**: Include a structured Markdown comparison table when evaluating options, tools, or strategies.
- **FAQ Section**: Provide a dedicated FAQ section addressing top user queries (Q&A format using H3 headings).
- **Conclusion**: Summary of core takeaways and an engaging closing CTA.

## 4. Quality & Readability Targets
- **Word Count**: Target 1,200 to 2,000 words for comprehensive pillar content.
- **Readability**: Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level 8 to 10 (clear, crisp language avoiding unnecessary jargon).
- **Formatting**: Bulleted lists, bold text for key terms, and blockquotes for expert tips or key takeaways.
